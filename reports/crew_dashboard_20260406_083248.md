# Engineering Brief: Fleet-Wide Anomaly

**Fleet Status: Red**

## Executive Summary

A critical, fleet-wide anomaly has been detected, with all six bridge assets reporting high-probability structural anomalies. The Anomaly Detection model is performing with high precision, indicating that these are not false positives and require immediate attention. 

The **Pacific Crown bridge ('bridge_alpha')** has been identified as the highest-risk asset, registering 129 distinct anomaly detections and a peak sensor-based Probability of Failure (PoF) of 0.71. All telemetry modalities (Predictions, Sensors, InSAR, GNSS) are in agreement, pointing to a dominant hotspot on the bridge's **Deck**.

A systemic issue is strongly suspected, as all bridges in the fleet are reporting a maximum prediction probability of 1.0. The primary feature driving the model across all assets is `Probability_of_Failure_PoF_roll_std_12`, suggesting a common underlying cause related to structural health instability.

## Maintenance Actions

The following actions are recommended based on the analysis.

### Immediate Actions (1)
- **Dispatch structural engineering team to Pacific Crown Bridge.** The report should focus on DECK integrity.

### Fleet-Wide Actions (1)
- **Notify Level 2 engineering of a suspected systemic issue.** Initiate a fleet-wide investigation to understand the cause of the systemic high probability anomalies.

### Scheduled Inspections (2)
- **Acknowledge and add Sound Span (Piers) and Hudson Relay (Deck)** to the priority maintenance queue for inspection after the immediate actions are complete.

### Maintenance Queue (3)
- **Add Lakeshore Axis, Gulf Meridian, and Atlantic Veil** to the standard maintenance queue for monitoring and eventual inspection.
